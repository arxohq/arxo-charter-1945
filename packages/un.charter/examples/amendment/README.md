# Why the Charter is so hard to amend

Worked cases over Articles 108-109 of the Charter. The norms stay in
`un.charter`. Expectations come from the letter of Article 108 (a two-thirds
adoption vote, a two-thirds ratification vote, and a named lock requiring
all five permanent members) and Article 109 (a convening vote that needs no
permanent-member concurrence at all, and an alteration that ends up ratified
under the same named lock as an ordinary amendment).

Same world throughout: fifteen Members — the five permanent members named by
Article 23, plus ten fictional Members — so two thirds is exactly ten states.
