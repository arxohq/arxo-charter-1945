# One dispute, two tracks

Worked cases over articles 33-38 and 52 of the Charter, following one
fictional dispute between two Member States stage by stage. Track A is what
the parties owe; track B is what the Security Council may do. Articles 34
and 37(2) name the Council as the assessor: until it supplies its finding
the engine answers `REQUIRES_JUDGMENT`, not a guess.

The norms stay in `un.charter`; expectations come from the text of Chapter
VI, article 52, and UNC-33–52.
