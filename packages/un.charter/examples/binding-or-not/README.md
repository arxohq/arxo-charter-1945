# Binding or not

Worked cases over Articles 10, 12, 18, 25 and 27 of the Charter, for a public
demo answering one question: what does the Charter bind a Member to, and
what does it not? The norms stay in `un.charter`; expectations here come
from the letter of those articles and from UNC-22/22B-D/30/30B/31/31B, not
from a fresh engine run.

A Security Council decision binds; a General Assembly recommendation does
not — that contrast, and the abstention that decides whether anything was
ever decided at all, are the point.
